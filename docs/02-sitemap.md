## Navigation

**Trust strip** (above the header): "Section 8 non-profit · Registered in Delhi", plus CIN and 80G once available, and the email address.

**Header (sticky):** Logo · About ▾ (About us, Team, Transparency) · Our work ▾ (the five areas) · Events · Stories · Get involved ▾ (Volunteer, Partner, Donate) · Contact · **Donate** button. On phones the menu opens as a side panel, and Donate moves to a bar fixed at the bottom of the screen. A floating WhatsApp button appears once a number is set.

**Footer:**
- Column 1: logo, one-line mission, "Registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013", CIN
- Column 2: links to the five focus areas
- Column 3: Get involved, Events, FAQ, Transparency
- Column 4: address, email, phone, WhatsApp, social media icons
- Newsletter signup (email only)
- Bottom bar: © year, Privacy · Terms · Refund policy

## Page by page

Each page lists its sections in order, its main call to action (CTA), and any form.

### Home `/`
1. Hero: name, one-line mission, photo. CTAs: **Donate** and **Volunteer**
2. Who we are: three or four lines, linking to About
3. Focus areas: five cards linking to `/our-work/*`
4. How your donation helps: three or four example amounts and what each pays for. The foundation must approve this text
5. Upcoming events: the next two or three. Hidden if there are none
6. Get involved strip: Volunteer · Partner · Donate
7. Trust strip: Section 8 registered, PAN, audited accounts, and 80G only once approved
8. Newsletter signup
- **Form:** Newsletter

### About `/about`
1. Our story: why the founders started the foundation
2. Vision and mission
3. Values (for example integrity, inclusion, community, transparency)
4. Legal identity box: legal name, Section 8 status, CIN, registered office, area of operation (all India)
5. CTA: links to Team and Transparency

### Team `/about/team`
A card for each person: photo, name, role and a short bio. Board first, then team and advisers. List only people who have agreed to appear.

### Our work `/our-work`
An introduction, then five cards, each with an icon, a summary and a link. CTA: Donate.

### Focus area pages `/our-work/[area]`
All five pages use the same template:
1. Hero: title and a one-line summary
2. The need: why this matters
3. What we do or plan to do: points from the MOA objects, rewritten in plain language
4. Programmes: cards, marked "coming soon" until real programmes exist
5. How you can help: donate to this cause, or volunteer. The donate link pre-selects the cause, for example `/donate?cause=education`
6. Related events

| Slug | Title | MOA |
|---|---|---|
| education | Education & Skills | 3(a) 1–3 |
| healthcare | Healthcare & Wellness | 3(a) 5 |
| social-welfare | Social Welfare | 3(a) 4 |
| culture-heritage | Culture & Heritage | 3(a) 4 |
| community-development | Community Development | 3(b) 1 |

### Events `/events`
Upcoming events show the date, title, venue, description and an optional registration link. Past events show photos. Examples: Ram Leela, festivals, health camps, workshops. In phase 1 the events come from a file in the code. In phase 4 they can be managed from the Google Sheet.

### Get involved `/get-involved`
Cards for Donate, Volunteer, Intern, Partner/CSR, and Spread the word (share buttons).

### Volunteer `/get-involved/volunteer`
Why volunteer, the areas where people can help and what to expect, then the form.
- **Form:** Volunteer / internship, saved to the Sheet tab `Volunteers`

### Partner `/get-involved/partner`
Who the foundation works with (companies giving CSR funds, institutions, NGOs, government) and what it offers. Registrations (CSR-1, 12A, 80G) once available. Then the form.
- **Form:** Partnership, saved to the Sheet tab `Partners`

### Donate `/donate`
1. A short appeal and trust points: secure payment, receipt by email, Section 8 non-profit
2. Donation form: amount buttons plus a custom amount, cause, donor details and declarations (see [06](06-forms-and-emails.md#donation-donate))
3. Payment: a UPI QR code with the amount and reference filled in, a "Pay with UPI app" button on phones, a bank transfer tab, then the transaction ID (UTR) field. Details in [04](04-donations.md)
4. Other ways to give: bank transfer or cheque details for large gifts, and a link for companies giving CSR funds
5. Short FAQ: tax, receipts, refunds, safety
- A `?cause=` link pre-selects the cause.

### Thank you `/donate/thank-you`
A thank-you message, the payment ID, "your receipt is on its way to your inbox", share buttons, and links to volunteering and events. Set to noindex so search engines skip it.

### Transparency `/transparency`
- Registration details and documents to download: Certificate of Incorporation, then 12A/80G and CSR-1 once available
- Governance: the board
- How funds are used
- Annual reports and audited accounts, once available
- FCRA statement: "We accept donations from Indian citizens only"

### FAQ `/faq`
Questions about donating (safety, receipts, tax benefit, refunds, cash, foreign donations), volunteering and partnerships. Uses FAQ structured data so answers can appear in Google results.

### Contact `/contact`
The form, registered office address, email, phone, WhatsApp, office hours and an embedded Google map.
- **Form:** Contact, saved to the Sheet tab `Contact`

### Policies
`/privacy-policy`, `/terms-and-conditions`, `/refund-policy`.
- India's data protection law requires a privacy policy, because the forms collect personal data.
- Drafts are written during the build. The foundation reviews them, ideally with its CA or lawyer.

## SEO

- Title pattern: `Page title | Jeevansphere Foundation`, with a unique meta description of up to 160 characters on each page.
- `sitemap.xml` is generated from the page list. Priority 1.0 for Home and Donate, 0.8 for main pages, 0.5 for policies.
- `robots.txt` allows everything except `/api/`. `/donate/thank-you` is marked noindex.
- Structured data: an `NGO` organisation schema on Home, `FAQPage` on the FAQ page, `Event` on the Events page.
- A preview image appears when a link is shared on WhatsApp, Facebook or LinkedIn.
- Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console at launch.
